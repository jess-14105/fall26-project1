Ghost Hunting Equipment

When attempting to contact the other side, investigators often use various devices that all look the same. Here is a small guide to some basic tools Sam and Colby use in their investigations:

EMF Reader (Electromagnetic Field Reader)

An EMF Reader is a handheld device that detects fluctuations in electromagnetic energy fields. Electromagnetic fields exist everywhere; some derive from electrical wiring and appliances (alternating current), while others from the earth itself (direct current). DCs cannot be detected by an EMF reader, but they can pick up ACs, which is what investigators believe the paranormal can manipulate. Industrial workers have used EMF readers for years to detect electrical radiation from power lines, cell towers, etc. They’ve also been used in medical and other scientific settings. However, it wasn’t until about 20 years ago that people started using them for ghost hunting.

To use an EMF reader for paranormal purposes, it is important to walk around the space you are investigating with the device to get a baseline read. This way, you’ll know what counts as an unusual spike in EMF intensity. Keep the device at waist level and keep it away from electrical wiring or devices to get a fair reading. Lastly, when you experience a high spike in energy, be sure to rule out any outside factors that can influence it.

Spirit Box

A spirit box is a small device that continually sweeps through AM/FM radio channels at a fast rate. This creates a constant white noise occasionally broken by sounds and voices. Some people believe that the words and phrases that spit out from radio frequencies are spirits communicating. When a spirit box goes through channels, it emits EMF energy, which is what spirits allegedly use to interact with the living.

To use a spirit box, make sure the device is on and sweeping through the radio of your choice (AM or FM). A faster speed is recommended to cut out most voices or music that would play on regular channels. After that, start asking questions! Since the spirit box can shoot out any words or sentences, you can ask open-ended questions as opposed to only yes or no.

Estes Method

A common technique Sam and Colby use with the spirit box is called the Estes Method. The spirit box is plugged into headphones that one person puts on; the volume is set quite high. They also wear a blindfold, effectively cutting out any outside noise and blocking their vision. This person is essentially put into a trance, where they can only hear the sweeping channels. Any word, phrase, or noise this person hears, they say out loud. The people outside of the spirit box ask questions, and if the words the person under the trance align with the questions asked, people think that a spirit is communicating with them.

REM Pod

A REM (Radiating Electromagnetism) Pod is a circular device with an antenna that creates its own small electromagnetic field. When a person or conductive material breaches this field, the small multicolor lights on top of the device top up and a noise emits. Some REM pods also have a temperature gage that goes off when there is a drastic change in temperature. Ghost enthusiasts believe that spirits can manipulate the EMF field to alert the living of their presence. However, the REM Pod is also quite sensitive, so it is best to keep all electrical devices away from the detector to eliminate any outside influence.
