The Hinsdale/Dandy House

Where is it?

The Hinsdale/Dandy House is located in Hinsdale, New York. The house resides in a secluded area in Cattaraugus County.

History

In 1853, David and Charles Everts purchased property in present-day Hinsdale, New York. Allegedly, David built what is now the Hinsdale House on a hilltop farm. Over the next few years, various people purchased the property; in 1861, Michael McMahon, who the road the house lives on was named after, bought the homestead. The McMahons loved the property for generations. In 1970, however, the Dandy family moved into the house, which is where the haunting began.

The Dandy family consisted of Phil, Clara, and their four children, Mike, Beth, Laura, and Mary. Since moving in, the Dandy’s experienced unexplainable activity in and around the house. When the events turned violent, they called in a priest, Father Trabold, to cleanse the house. Unfortunately, the cleansing was unsuccessful, and in 1974, the Dandy family moved out. After, another family lived and passed in the house, and in 2015, the current owner, Daniel Klaes, purchased the house and now holds paranormal tours and investigations.

Kreischer Mansion

Where is it?

The Kreischer Mansion is located in Charleston, Staten Island, New York.

History

In 1835, a large fire burned down hundreds of wooden buildings in New York City, about 17 blocks. One year later, Balthasar Kreischer arrived in New York from Germany with the hopes of helping the city get back on its feet. In 1854, he started a brick manufacturing factory to help rebuild. He started on the Lower East side but moved to Staten Island in 1874, where his impact was so great on the community; they named the town he lived in after him: Kreischerville. Kreischer built two mansions side by side across the street from the factory, and had Charles and Edward, two of six of his children, inherit them. After Kreischer’s passing in 1886, one of them burned down, but the other still stands today.

In 1894, Edward Kreischer allegedly took his own life behind the brick factory. Reports however, claim that Edward was in a good mood that day and had no reasons to commit suicide. The case remains open as people claim that he was murdered. Supposedly, Charles, Edward, and their older brother George were on ill terms; they had run the brick business together after their father’s death. There are also mentions of Edward’s wife, Frieda, having an affair with their doctor before his death.

The house remained abandoned for over 100 years, until it was renovated as a restaurant in the 1990s and 2000s. However, it simultaneously became a meeting place for mob members. The caretaker of the house, Joe Black, was also a hitman. The Bonanno family, who were part of the mafia, kept ties with Black. In 2005, they placed a hit on Robert McKelvey, who bragged around town that he owed money to the mafia. On March 29th, Black lured McKelvey into the mansion, stabbed and drowned him, then incinerated his body.

Today, the mansion is open to paranormal investigations.

Evidence

The Saratoga County Homestead

Where is it?

Saratoga County Homestead is located in Barkersville, NY. The Homestead resides on a lone hill in Saratoga County.

History

The Homestead Sanitorium opened in 1914 amid the Tuberculosis crisis. As cases increased in New York however, the Homestead expanded in 1936 to feature decorative features and massive windows to let in the fresh mountain air, which people thought was vital to treatment at the time. There were strict rules at the Sanitorium; bedrest was mandatory, exercise was regulated, and dancing, cigarettes, and handkerchiefs were prohibited.

As Tuberculosis phased out, the Sanitorium closed in 1960 and reopened as an Infirmary. The Homestead treated patients until it closed its doors for good in 1973. The building remained abandoned for 45 years until it was purchased in 2019. Today, the place is open for paranormal investigations.

Evidence

Sam and Colby visited the Saratoga County Homestead in August 2021. While there, they encountered many intelligent and sinister spirits roaming in the halls. The most peculiar part of the investigation is when the music box, or motion sensor, goes off when Josh tells a spirit to stand in front of it.

Shanley Hotel

Where is it?

The Shanley Hotel is located in Napanoch, NY. Napanoch rests in the foothills of Shawangunk Mountain in Ulster County.

History

In 1845, Thomas Ridge built the Thomas Ridge Hotel in Napanoch, NY. At the time, it was claimed to be one of the fanciest and richest hotels in the area. Ridge sold the hotel in 1851 to Mr. Hungerford; he renamed it to the Hungerford Hotel. After changing ownership a few times, Adolph Wagner bought the hotel in 1887. In March 1895, a nearby home caught fire and spread to the hotel; it burned the building to the ground. Wagner did not want his business to go to waste, so he quickly rebuilt it and reopened the establishment that same year.

In 1906, James Shanley, who immigrated from Ireland, bought the hotel and aptly renamed it the Shanley Hotel. He upgraded the place by adding accoutrements such as a bowling alley and a barbershop. James and his wife, Beatrice, welcomed guests of high nobility such as Thomas Edison and Eleanor Roosevelt for years. Tragedy started in the family when Beatrice gave birth in 1912 to Kathleen, who soon passed, followed by two other children who died young. Many deaths connected to the Shanley family followed, including Beatrice’s sister and the Barber’s daughter Rosie. After the 18th Amendment passed, Shanley opened a Speakeasy/Gentlemen’s Club and a Bordello within the hotel.

After James died of a heart attack in 1937, Beatrice sold the hotel to Allen H. Hazen, who ran the business until he passed in 1971. After changing ownership a few more times, it was abandoned in 1991 for 10 years. In 2005, the building was rediscovered, and today, it serves as an existing hotel and a place of paranormal investigation.

Evidence

Sam and Colby visited the Shanley Hotel in July 2021. There, they encountered the paranormal through manipulation of their equipment and physical objects. The highlight of the night is when a soccer ball moves from a room and into the hallway, followed by a door starting to close and reopen.
